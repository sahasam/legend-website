This is the first thing I am writing here. It exists mainly so the site has
something to load — but also as a kind of plant-the-flag moment. Most of the
ideas I want to write down are still in their soft, unfinished state. Like deep
sea creatures, they don't survive being yanked too quickly to the surface.

## what this site is

A portfolio, eventually. A notebook, mostly. The visual mood — drifting layers,
soft glow, a slow current — is on purpose. I want it to feel less like a
résumé and more like a place you wander into.

## what to expect

- short essays
- half-formed thoughts I am thinking through in public
- the occasional thing I have built

If you're reading this: thanks for descending.
